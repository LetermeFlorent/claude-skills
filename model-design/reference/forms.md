# Forms and surfaces

## Masses that make a body read as alive

| Zone | What to build |
|---|---|
| Head | skull mass, eye socket under a brow ridge, cheek, jaw muscle behind the eye, lower jaw as its own mass, nostrils at the tip |
| Neck | thicker where it meets the chest, muscle running from the back of the skull to the shoulders |
| Chest | ribcage as an egg wider than the hips, keel or sternum in front for winged animals |
| Shoulder | shoulder blade lying on the ribcage, upper arm muscle bulging over it |
| Belly | softer, hanging slightly below the ribcage line |
| Pelvis | hip bones visible under the skin, thigh muscle from there to the knee |
| Legs | knee and ankle as bony bulges, muscle high, tendons thin near the foot |
| Tail | continues the spine, round at the base, flatter or ridged toward the tip |

Limbs are never cylinders: thick at the body, tapering, swelling at each joint, flattened where tendons
run. Two sides of the body differ slightly in the final pass.

## Surfaces from real references

| Surface | Real reference | Pattern and relief |
|---|---|---|
| Back armour | crocodile osteoderms, armadillo | raised plates in rows along the spine, each with a ridge, gaps between them dark and deep |
| Body scales | lizards, snakes | small on joints and face, large on back and limbs, overlapping toward the tail |
| Belly | snake and crocodile belly scutes | wide transverse bands, lighter, smoother |
| Wing membrane | bat wing | thin, veins branching from the finger bones, fine wrinkles near the body, light passing through |
| Horn | ram, rhino, antelope | growth rings across, grooves along, dark base, pale worn tip |
| Claw, tooth | raptor claw, crocodile tooth | smooth, darker at the root, chipped or worn at the tip |
| Hide on joints | elephant, rhino | deep folds where the limb bends |
| Wear | any old animal | scars crossing the pattern, chipped plate edges, dirt in crevices and on the feet |

## Objects

Edges bevelled, never razor sharp; wear on edges and corners people touch; dirt where water and dust
collect; each material keeps its own grain or pattern at real-world scale.
