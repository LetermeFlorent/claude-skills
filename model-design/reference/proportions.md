# Proportions

Starting points from common anatomy and art practice, not measurements of one animal: copy the closest
table into the design sheet, then change values on purpose. Unit = head length (tip of snout or nose to
back of skull) unless the table says otherwise.

## Human

| Part | Heads |
|---|---|
| Total height | 7.5 realistic, 8 heroic |
| Shoulder width | 2 (man), 1.75 (woman) |
| Chin to nipples, nipples to navel, navel to crotch | 1 each |
| Crotch to knee, knee to sole | 2 each |
| Upper arm, forearm, hand | 1.5, 1.25, 0.75 |

Joints: neck, shoulder, elbow, wrist, fingers (3 each), spine (3 or more), hip, knee, ankle, toes, jaw.

## Big cat, dog, wolf (four legs, digitigrade)

| Part | Heads |
|---|---|
| Neck | 1 |
| Shoulder to hip | 3 |
| Height at shoulder | 3 |
| Tail | cat 3 to 3.5, wolf 2 |
| Front leg: upper, lower, foot | 1, 1, 0.5 |
| Hind leg: thigh, shin, foot (heel off the ground) | 1, 1.2, 0.8 |

Joints: jaw, neck (2 or more), shoulder blade, shoulder, elbow, wrist, toes, spine (3 or more), hip, knee,
heel, toes, tail (5 or more).

## Horse, deer (four legs, hooves)

| Part | Heads |
|---|---|
| Neck | 1.5 |
| Chest to buttock | 2.5 |
| Height at withers | 2.5 to 2.7 |
| Leg length to body depth | about 1 to 1 |

## Crocodile, lizard

| Part | Share of total length |
|---|---|
| Head | 1/7 |
| Neck and body | 2/7 |
| Tail | 1/2 or more |
| Legs | short, elbows and knees out to the sides |

## Bird of prey

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| Wingspan | 2.2 to 2.5 body lengths |
| Wing bones | upper arm, forearm, hand; feathers carry most of the span |

## Bat wing (used for most dragon and wyvern wings)

Upper arm, forearm, then a thumb with a claw and four long fingers; the third finger is the longest, about
the forearm. Membrane spans between the fingers and down to the hind leg or the body side, slightly slack
between the bones. Joints: shoulder, elbow, wrist, each finger at its base and middle.

## Wyvern or dragon (invented, built from the tables above)

| Part | Heads |
|---|---|
| Neck | 1.5 to 2 |
| Chest to hip | 3 |
| Tail | 4 to 5, thick at the base, half the base width by its middle |
| Hind leg: thigh, shin, foot | 1.2, 1.4, 0.9 (bird or cat stance) |
| Wingspan | 8 to 10 |
| Wing arm when used as foreleg | upper arm 1.5, forearm 2, fingers 3 to 4 |

Joints: jaw, neck (4 or more), spine (3 or more), tail (6 or more), shoulder, elbow, wrist, wing fingers,
hip, knee, ankle, toes.

## Objects

Measure against one known part: seat height 0.45 m, door 2 m, step 0.18 m, hand grip 0.03 to 0.04 m,
car wheel 0.6 to 0.7 m. Real-world size first, then the style may push it.
